Who we are

Calmaria is made by Steale LLC (“Steale”, “we”, “us”). This policy explains what happens to information when you use the Calmaria apps for iPhone, Android, Apple Watch and Wear OS, the Calmaria web app, and this website (together, “Calmaria”).

The short version: the apps are built so that we never receive your personal information. The sections below explain exactly what that means, and where the website differs.

What stays on your device

When you use the Calmaria apps, the following is created and stored locally on your own device. We have no copy of it and no way to see it:

  • your breathing rhythms, including any custom rhythms you create
  • your session history: which rhythm you used, how many breaths, and when
  • mood check-ins: how you said you felt after a session
  • your settings, such as theme, sound and vibration
  • simple statistics calculated from the above, such as your total sessions

This information remains on your device until you delete it, delete the app, or clear the web app’s site data. Your device’s own protections, such as its passcode and storage encryption, apply to it.

What we don’t collect

The Calmaria apps do not collect, transmit, sell, rent or share personal information. They contain no advertising, no advertising identifiers, no third-party analytics or crash-reporting tools, and no tracking of any kind. You don’t create an account, and we never ask for your name, email address, phone number, location or contacts.

On Android the app requests only one permission: vibration, so it can pulse in time with your breath. It does not request access to your location, contacts, microphone, camera or storage.

Apple Health (iPhone and Apple Watch)

If you choose to, Calmaria can connect to the Health app on your iPhone. When you grant permission, Calmaria saves your completed sessions to Health as mindful minutes and workouts, and it can read mindfulness and workout data back to show your progress and statistics.

This is entirely optional and controlled by you. Health data is stored and protected by Apple on your device, and, depending on your own settings, in your iCloud account. Calmaria never sends Health data to us or to anyone else, and never uses it for advertising or marketing. You can change or withdraw Calmaria’s access at any time in Settings → Health → Data Access & Devices.

Syncing between your devices

Calmaria on Apple Watch and Wear OS stays in step with Calmaria on your phone, so your rhythms, theme and settings match. This sync happens directly between your own devices using the system services provided by Apple (Watch Connectivity) and Google (the Wearable Data Layer). Calmaria does not run a server, and the information does not pass through one of ours.

The iPhone widget reads the theme and a few summary figures from storage that is shared between the app and the widget on the same device.

The web app and this website

The web app stores your rhythms, history, mood check-ins and settings in your browser’s local storage on your device. They are not uploaded. Clearing your browser’s site data for Calmaria removes them, and so does using a different browser or device (they don’t follow you).

This website and the web app do use some third-party services, which is different from the apps:

  • Google Analytics. The website and web app load Google Analytics to tell us, in aggregate, how many people visit and which pages are used. Google may set cookies or similar identifiers and receives technical information such as your IP address, browser and device type, the pages you view, and your approximate location. Google’s use of that information is described in its own privacy policy. You can block this with your browser’s privacy settings, a content blocker, or Google’s opt-out browser add-on.
  • Google Fonts. The site loads typefaces from Google Fonts, which means your browser makes a request to Google’s servers that includes your IP address.
  • Theme preference. If you switch between light and dark on this site, we remember your choice in your browser’s local storage. It never leaves your browser.
  • App stores. Links to the App Store and Google Play take you to services operated by Apple and Google, which have their own privacy policies.

We use website analytics only to understand and improve the site. We do not combine it with anything else, and we do not sell it.

How we use information, and why

For the apps, there is nothing to use: we don’t receive your information. For the website, where analytics applies, we rely on our legitimate interest in understanding how the site is used so that we can keep it clear and useful, and, where the law requires it, on your consent. We don’t make decisions about you using automated profiling.

Sharing

We do not sell your personal information and we do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising. We have no advertising partners. The only parties that receive website visit information are the service providers named above, acting under their own terms. We may disclose information if required by law, but since the apps don’t give us your personal data, we would have nothing about you to disclose from them.

How long we keep things

App data lives on your device for as long as you keep it. Website analytics information is retained by Google according to the retention settings we configure and Google’s own policies. We don’t keep any other personal information.

Your choices and rights

Because the apps keep everything on your device, you are already in control of it. You can delete your session history and custom rhythms inside the app, remove the app to erase its data, clear the web app’s site data, and withdraw Health access in your device settings at any time.

Depending on where you live (for example the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or California), you may have the right to ask what personal information a company holds about you, to correct or delete it, to object to or restrict its use, and to complain to your local data protection authority. For the small amount of website visitor information described above, you can exercise those rights by contacting us. We do not discriminate against anyone for exercising their privacy rights.

Children

Calmaria is a general breathing app and is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the website, please contact us and we will delete it.

Security

The strongest protection is the one built into the design: information that we never receive can’t be lost from our systems. Data on your device is protected by your device’s own security features. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, so we can’t guarantee absolute security, but we take care to keep Calmaria simple and small.

International visitors

Steale LLC is based in the United States, and our website analytics provider may process information in the United States and other countries. Where required, we rely on the safeguards that provider offers for international transfers.

Changes to this policy

If we change how Calmaria handles information, we will update this page and the date at the top. If a change would reduce your privacy, we will say so clearly here and, where practical, in the app’s release notes.
