Questions? Answers.

What does Calmaria mean?

In Portuguese, calmaria (/kɑʊma’ɾia/) means calm and stillness. Sailors and surfers use it for the moment the wind drops and the ocean goes flat. That’s the feeling this is for.

Is Calmaria really free?

Yes. No subscriptions, no ads, no accounts, no upsell. Calmaria is here to help you breathe, not to keep you looking.

Which rhythm should you start with?

If you’re not sure, start with 4-7-8: in for four, hold for seven, out for eight. It’s a gentle way to wind down. Box breathing (5-5-5-5) is good when you need to focus. Choose whatever feels comfortable, and if anything feels strained, stop and breathe normally.

Does the web app do everything the phone apps do?

Yes. The web app has all five themes, your own rhythms, sound, mood check-ins and history. Add it to your home screen and it feels like any other app.

Where is my data kept?

On your devices. Your history lives on your phone and watch and syncs between them through Apple’s and Google’s own services, not a Calmaria account. We don’t run a server that receives it. Read the privacy policy for backups and how to delete your history.
