Questions? Answers.

What does Calmaria mean?

In Portuguese, calmaria (/kɑʊma’ɾia/) means calmness and tranquility—used by sailors and surfers when there are no waves in the ocean.

Is Calmaria really free with no subscriptions?

Yes. Calmaria is completely free across iOS, Android, Watch, and Web. There are no subscriptions, no ads, and no accounts.

Does the Web App have the same features as the iOS and Android apps?

Yes. The Web App includes the responsive two-column layout, all five visual themes (Circles, Glass, 80s, 3D Dots, and Angle Gradient), custom presets, Cuelume synthesized sound, mood check-ins, and session history.

Where is my breathing data stored?

Strictly on your device. Calmaria never uploads your presets, session history, or mood check-ins to any server. Read the Privacy Policy.
